We developed BEHA³VE for the benchmark track of the Workshop on Agent Behavior at COLM 2026, building on our ABxLab shopping experiments and interventions.

## Related paper

If you find BEHA³VE useful in your research, please cite our ABxLab paper:

> Manuel Cherep, Chengtian Ma, Abigail Xu, Maya Shaked, Pattie Maes, and Nikhil Singh. A Framework for Studying AI Agent Behavior: Evidence from Consumer Choice Experiments. ICLR 2026.

```bibtex
@inproceedings{cherep2026abxlab,
  title={A Framework for Studying AI Agent Behavior: Evidence from Consumer Choice Experiments},
  author={Cherep, Manuel and Ma, Chengtian and Xu, Abigail and Shaked, Maya and Maes, Pattie and Singh, Nikhil},
  booktitle={The Fourteenth International Conference on Learning Representations},
  year={2026},
  url={https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25609}
}
```

In this paper, we evaluated agents in shopping environments. BEHA³VE extends that work to additional environments and experimental designs.
